---
title: Focus board
description: Organize weekly goals and to-dos beside your agents.
---

Open the focus board with the **▦** icon in the sidebar footer (**B** when Nerd Font glyphs are off). Hover for the “Focus board” tooltip. Click the icon again, or press `Esc`, to close it.

The board has **Draft**, **To Do**, **In Progress**, and **Done** columns. Draft is for ideas that agents never pick up. Press `f` to cycle the view-only area filter through All, scalable, harness, and personal. The header shows a ◉ icon and the active area in plain text. Cards in every column and goals without a matching to-do are hidden; the filter is not written to Obsidian. New cards use the selected area, or harness when the filter is All. Press `n` from any column to add a Draft card, then press `Ctrl+Enter` or click **Save**. Press `g` or click **+ goal** to create a goal with a week, today, or container scope; each line in its to-do field becomes a Draft card in the filtered area.

Select cards with the arrow keys; `1`–`4` jump to Draft, To Do, In Progress, and Done. Press `m` then `←` or `→` to move a selected card. Mouse drag also moves cards between columns. `Enter` or a click opens card detail. Press `e` to edit the selected card in zen mode, or `z` to open zen mode directly; with no card selected, `z` starts a new Draft card. Zen mode shows the title and Human text editor only. `Esc` saves and returns to the same board selection; `Ctrl+S` saves while staying in zen mode, `Tab` switches between title and Human text, and `n` saves the current card and starts another Draft card. Press `?` to see the complete board shortcut list. The Human tab holds editable raw text; the Agent tab holds a summary and append-only updates. In Progress groups cards by the worst state of their linked agents: blocked, working, then done awaiting you.

On a card without an agent in To Do, In Progress, or Done, click **▶ spawn** or press `s`. Herdr opens an agent with the card title and description as its prompt, then links the new terminal. Scalable uses the configured `scalablev2` checkout, harness uses `scalable-agent-fleet`, and personal uses the Obsidian vault. Each linked terminal shows its host, status dot, and the first words of its latest line. Select it and press `Enter`, or click it, to jump to the pane; `Esc` returns to the board.

Goals and to-dos live in one Markdown note per ISO week under `<Obsidian vault>/todos/YYYY-Www.md`. Herdr uses `OBSIDIAN_VAULT` when set, otherwise `~/workspaces/obsidian`. Goal headings and to-do checkboxes remain readable and editable in Obsidian. Reopen the board to read external edits. A goal checks off only when all its linked to-dos are Done. Herdr refuses to overwrite a note changed while the board was open.

If the goals strip has more goals than fit, use its arrow controls or `[` and `]` to page through them.
