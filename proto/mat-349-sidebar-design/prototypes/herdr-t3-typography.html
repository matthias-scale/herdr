summary

Herdr keeps its terminal grid, so this does not copy T3 pixel for pixel. It copies T3's text hierarchy: regular-weight body text, one muted grey for metadata, bold only for headings and the focused row, links in blue with no underline, inline code as a shaded chip, brand icons before links, and air between groups. Font and line height come from T3's own settings: SF Mono, 1.3 line height. Left is today, right is the proposal. Brand icons render from the Herdr Icons font in the terminal; this page approximates them with coloured glyphs.

sidebar

Titles in regular weight, ages right-aligned in muted grey, group headers muted, one blank row between groups, one dot glyph with colour carrying the state. Bold marks only the focused row.
